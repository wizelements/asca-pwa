5. Agreement Terms

Website-ready optimized photos only. Client-supplied images must be web-optimized JPG/PNG/WebP. Raw camera files, PSDs, video, or print-resolution assets are not included. The agency may recompress or resize images for performance.

Raw photo backup storage is not included. The monthly fee covers live, admin-managed images displayed on the site. Offline backups of original files are the client’s responsibility unless a separate backup add-on is purchased.

Third-party platform fees pass through at cost. Domain registration, Vercel Pro (if needed), Turso database overages, Resend email volume, and any future image CDN or payment-processor fees are billed to the client at actual cost.

Major new features are quoted separately. Anything not included above — such as newsletter campaigns, payment/membership integration, custom admin roles, public member directory, push notifications, or migration to object storage — will be quoted before work begins.

Billing. Service is billed monthly in advance. The first month is due upon signing. Cancellation requires 30 days written notice.
